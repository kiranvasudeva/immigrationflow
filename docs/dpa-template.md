# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Template

**Between:** ImmigrationFlow (Data Processor)
**And:** [Client Company Name] (Data Controller)

## 1. Definitions

**"Data Controller"** means the Client who determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

**"Data Processor"** means ImmigrationFlow, which processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined in GDPR Article 4(1).

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal data as defined in GDPR Article 4(2).

## 2. Subject Matter and Purpose

### 2.1 Subject Matter
This DPA governs the processing of personal data by ImmigrationFlow when providing Romanian immigration workflow management services.

### 2.2 Purpose
Processing is limited to:
- Managing immigration case workflows
- Document collection and processing
- Status tracking and reporting
- Communication with relevant parties
- Compliance with Romanian immigration law

### 2.3 Duration
This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the Service Agreement and until all personal data is returned or deleted.

## 3. Categories of Data and Data Subjects

### 3.1 Data Subjects
- Company employees (foreign workers)
- Legal representatives
- Company contacts and administrators

### 3.2 Categories of Personal Data
- **Identity Data:** Names, nationality, date of birth
- **Contact Data:** Email addresses, phone numbers, addresses
- **Official Documents:** Passport details, work permits, visas
- **Employment Data:** Job titles, salary information, contract details
- **Document Metadata:** Upload timestamps, processing status

### 3.3 Special Categories
- **Criminal Background Checks:** If required by Romanian law
- **Medical Certificates:** Health examination results
- **Biometric Data:** If contained in passport or visa photos

## 4. Processor Obligations

### 4.1 General Obligations
ImmigrationFlow shall:
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Ensure confidentiality of all persons authorized to process personal data
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Respect conditions for engaging sub-processors
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests
- Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with GDPR obligations
- Delete or return personal data upon termination of services
- Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance

### 4.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption:** AES-256 encryption for data at rest and in transit
- **Access Control:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
- **Monitoring:** Continuous security monitoring and logging
- **Incident Response:** Documented breach notification procedures
- **Regular Testing:** Penetration testing, vulnerability assessments

### 4.3 Staff Requirements
- Background checks for all personnel with access to personal data
- Regular training on GDPR compliance and data protection
- Confidentiality agreements with all staff members
- Clear roles and responsibilities for data protection

## 5. Sub-Processing

### 5.1 General Authorization
The Controller provides general authorization for ImmigrationFlow to engage sub-processors, subject to the conditions below.

### 5.2 Sub-Processor Requirements
- Written contract with equivalent data protection obligations
- Regular audits and compliance monitoring
- EU/EEA location or adequate safeguards
- Prior notification to Controller of changes

### 5.3 Current Sub-Processors
- **Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany):** Cloud infrastructure and hosting
- **Mailjet SAS (France):** Email delivery services
- **[Other EU-based service providers as applicable]**

### 5.4 Sub-Processor Changes
- 30 days advance notice for new or changed sub-processors
- Controller may object on reasonable grounds
- Alternative solutions provided if objection sustained

## 6. Data Transfers

### 6.1 Location Restrictions
- Primary processing within EU/EEA
- Romanian servers for regulatory compliance
- Third-country transfers only with appropriate safeguards

### 6.2 Transfer Mechanisms
- **EU/EEA:** Standard processing
- **Third Countries:** Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions
- **Emergency Access:** Limited to EU-based support staff

## 7. Data Subject Rights

### 7.1 Assistance Obligations
ImmigrationFlow shall assist the Controller in responding to:
- Access requests (Article 15 GDPR)
- Rectification requests (Article 16 GDPR)
- Erasure requests (Article 17 GDPR)
- Restriction requests (Article 18 GDPR)
- Portability requests (Article 20 GDPR)
- Objection requests (Article 21 GDPR)

### 7.2 Response Procedures
- Acknowledge requests within 48 hours
- Provide necessary data and documentation
- Implement corrections or deletions as instructed
- Document all actions taken

## 8. Data Breach Procedures

### 8.1 Notification Requirements
- Immediate notification to Controller (within 24 hours of discovery)
- Detailed incident report within 72 hours
- Ongoing updates during investigation and remediation

### 8.2 Breach Information
- Nature and categories of data affected
- Approximate number of data subjects and records
- Likely consequences and mitigation measures
- Contact point for further information

### 8.3 Investigation and Remediation
- Immediate containment measures
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions and timeline
- Prevention measures for future incidents

## 9. Data Protection Impact Assessments

### 9.1 Assistance Obligation
ImmigrationFlow shall assist the Controller in conducting DPIAs when required, including:
- Technical and organizational measures implemented
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Consultation on high-risk processing activities

## 10. Audits and Compliance

### 10.1 Audit Rights
- Controller may conduct audits with reasonable notice
- Third-party audits acceptable with appropriate confidentiality
- Annual compliance reports provided
- Immediate access in case of suspected breach

### 10.2 Compliance Documentation
- Processing records maintained per GDPR Article 30
- Security measures documentation
- Staff training records
- Sub-processor agreements and monitoring

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

### 11.1 Retention Periods
- Active cases: Duration of immigration process plus 2 years
- Completed cases: 7 years for legal compliance
- Audit logs: 10 years for regulatory requirements

### 11.2 Deletion Procedures
- Secure deletion using NIST 800-88 guidelines
- Certificate of destruction provided
- Backup data purged according to schedule
- Exception for legal hold requirements

## 12. Liability and Indemnification

### 12.1 Processor Liability
ImmigrationFlow is liable for damages caused by processing that:
- Does not comply with GDPR obligations specifically directed to processors
- Acts outside or contrary to lawful instructions from the Controller

### 12.2 Limitation of Liability
- Liability limited to direct damages
- Aggregate liability cap as defined in Service Agreement
- Mutual indemnification for regulatory fines

## 13. Term and Termination

### 13.1 Term
This DPA takes effect upon execution and continues until termination of the Service Agreement.

### 13.2 Termination Procedures
- 90 days notice for data return/deletion
- Choice of secure deletion or data return
- Certification of deletion provided
- Exception for legal retention requirements

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

### 14.1 Governing Law
This DPA is governed by the laws of Romania and European Union data protection law.

### 14.2 Dispute Resolution
- Good faith negotiation for 30 days
- Mediation through EU-approved mediator
- Jurisdiction of Romanian courts
- Emergency injunctive relief available

## 15. Amendments

### 15.1 Amendment Process
- Mutual written consent required
- Updates for legal/regulatory changes
- 30 days notice for material changes
