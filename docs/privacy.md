# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [Date]

## Introduction

ImmigrationFlow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Romanian immigration management platform.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Client Information:** Company name, CUI, ONRC, CAEN code, business address, contact details
- **Worker Information:** Name, nationality, passport details, contact information, date of birth
- **Authentication Data:** Email addresses, encrypted passwords, session information
- **Document Data:** Uploaded files, document metadata, processing history

### Technical Information
- **Usage Data:** Log files, IP addresses, browser information, device identifiers
- **Performance Data:** System performance metrics, error logs, user interactions
- **Security Data:** Login attempts, security events, audit trails

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purposes
- **Service Delivery:** Managing immigration workflows, document processing, status tracking
- **Communication:** Sending notifications, reminders, status updates, support responses
- **Compliance:** Meeting legal requirements, generating reports for Romanian authorities
- **Security:** Protecting against fraud, unauthorized access, ensuring data integrity

### Secondary Purposes
- **Service Improvement:** Analyzing usage patterns, optimizing user experience
- **Support:** Providing technical assistance, troubleshooting issues
- **Business Operations:** Internal reporting, quality assurance, training

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on:
- **Contract Performance:** Processing necessary for service delivery
- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with Romanian immigration laws
- **Legitimate Interest:** Service improvement, security, fraud prevention
- **Consent:** Marketing communications (where applicable)

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Authorized Recipients
- **Romanian Authorities:** IGI, AJOFM/ANOFM, consulates (as required by law)
- **Legal Representatives:** Authorized immigration lawyers or consultants
- **Service Providers:** EU-based cloud hosting, email services, payment processors

### Disclosure Circumstances
- **Legal Requirements:** Court orders, regulatory requests, law enforcement
- **Business Operations:** Mergers, acquisitions (with confidentiality protections)
- **Emergency Situations:** Protecting health, safety, or legal rights

## Data Retention

### Retention Periods
- **Active Cases:** Duration of immigration process plus 2 years
- **Completed Cases:** 7 years from case completion
- **Authentication Data:** 2 years from last login
- **Audit Logs:** 10 years for compliance purposes

### Deletion Procedures
- Secure deletion using industry-standard methods
- Certificate of destruction available upon request
- Backup data purged according to retention schedules

## Data Security

### Technical Measures
- **Encryption:** AES-256 encryption for data at rest and in transit
- **Access Control:** Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication
- **Infrastructure:** EU-based servers, ISO 27001 certified data centers
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring, intrusion detection systems

### Organizational Measures
- **Staff Training:** Regular privacy and security training
- **Access Management:** Principle of least privilege, regular access reviews
- **Incident Response:** Documented procedures, breach notification protocols
- **Vendor Management:** Due diligence, data processing agreements

## Your Rights (GDPR)

### Data Subject Rights
- **Access:** Request copies of your personal data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Erasure:** Request deletion (subject to legal obligations)
- **Portability:** Receive data in machine-readable format
- **Restriction:** Limit processing in certain circumstances
- **Objection:** Opt-out of processing based on legitimate interest

### Exercising Rights
- **Contact:** privacy@immigrationflow.com
- **Response Time:** 30 days (may be extended by 60 days for complex requests)
- **Identity Verification:** Required to prevent unauthorized disclosure
- **Fees:** Generally free; reasonable fees for excessive requests

## International Data Transfers

### Transfer Mechanisms
- **EU/EEA:** Standard processing within European Economic Area
- **Third Countries:** Adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses
- **Safeguards:** Binding Corporate Rules, certification schemes

### Specific Transfers
- **Email Services:** Mailjet EU (France) - GDPR compliant
- **Cloud Storage:** Hetzner (Germany) - EU-based infrastructure
- **Document Processing:** LibreOffice (self-hosted EU servers)

## Cookies and Tracking
